## Priority decisions

- **Retain SEC-03 as High.** The legacy recorder has a confirmed cancellation gap at the source level. A pending permission request can outlive the workflow that requested it, then start capture without the originating controls. Runtime feasibility depends on permission timing and lifecycle events; no physical microphone or delayed-grant run on this path was performed in this review. This does not establish background capture on all browsers, covert permission bypass, or transmission of recordings.
- **Narrow SEC-01 to Medium for the assessed private app.** An untrusted snapshot can reach attributes without adequate validation or encoding. The weakness is confirmed in the source, while the effect of a hostile import and deployed controls remain unmeasured. The normal trigger requires an owner to accept a restore or already have an unsafe stored snapshot. Keep remediation near the top of the roadmap regardless of numerical severity. Reassess severity if user-to-user backup exchange or a more exposed trust boundary is established.
- **Treat D1-01 and D1-02 as one asset-reference invariant with two evidence levels.** Artwork loss under ZIP replacement was observed by the data lane and independently verified in source. Restore independently retained omitted retirement metadata in a normal synthetic Store fixture. The complete sequence leading to audio loss remains a conditional High-consequence risk, not an observed audio-loss incident. Design the first fix to prevent both without charging the backlog twice for one reference-safety rule.
- **Promote D1-03 from static prediction to reproduced Medium.** On the running app, ordinary Undo at a later cue persisted a rewind across reload. The retained coverage shows why this should be described as resume-position loss, not loss of all progress.
- **Retain UX-06, ARC-03 and PB-01–04.** UX-06 occurs before actual streaming becomes relevant. ARC-03 concerns the offline metadata shell. Playback findings are bounded by a specific mode change, session accounting, premature source EOF or a conforming caption style block. No Critical issue was established by this review.

## System and product trade-offs

- **Accept scoped invariants and transaction ordering.** A shared lesson-removal operation, reference-safe asset retirement, consistent playback scope and a bounded navigation commit address observed behavior. Reuse the existing adapters, DOM and Worker. A rewritten router, framework migration, replacement datastore or new native host are unsupported by this evidence.
- **Accept the mechanics of consolidation only when it preserves separate purposes.** The duplicate status pair is redundant. Consolidating common microphone cancellation/release with a small shared lifecycle is a plausible follow-up after the immediate legacy fix. Removing the untimed record/download entry point is unsupported, because it still serves untimed and YouTube-only lessons where Mirror is unavailable. Do not remove history, category tools or advanced transport based on personal preference. [Feature disposition](product-strategist.md#feature-value-and-disposition)
- **Treat test coverage and production certainty separately.** Preserve passing baseline assertions and repair drift in current UI entry points without weakening what they assert. Browser fakes, Node SQLite and synthetic R2 establish local contracts. They leave actual Sites identity enforcement, production quotas, native device permissions, mobile battery, real YouTube streams and PWA installs unassessed. No unresolved item here justifies requiring a rewrite as a prerequisite to repairing known behavior.
- **Acceptance roadmap scope is proportionate.** The eight proposed initiatives in the [roadmap](../roadmap.md) separate privacy and data integrity from transport behavior, UI interaction and performance. QA can restore reliable execution before product fixes, with known product defects remaining visible as failing regressions until addressed. Performance targets are budgets to test on the same fixture, not results already achieved. Mirror must wait for the complete original before **starting recording**. The normal permission request may precede the original. Hosted validation should check trusted identity injection and access enforcement on the packaged route, plus bindings and migrations. No artifact signing mechanism was established in this baseline.
