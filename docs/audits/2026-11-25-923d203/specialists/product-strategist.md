# Product strategy and learning journeys

**Assignment:** ECHO-T1 · Product Strategist · audit · `work` at `923d203f4d303b838cfec64ffc52686fe8f8c335`

Echo Studio already supports a coherent listen, repeat and return loop. The strongest pieces are personal recordings with source-aligned cues, frictionless resumption, and discreet tools for repeating a difficult phrase. The largest product opportunity is making the current loop dependable and the progress it reports meaningful. A mode that claims to play the full recording must keep doing so after a filtered session. A learner should be able to trust where the app resumes, which phrases were heard in this session, and whether a lesson or recording remains available.

**Product coherence scores 7/10 with moderate confidence.** Source and specialist results establish the shape of the product and several practical failures. The report assesses workflow quality from current requirements, entry points and observed behaviour. Learning gains, ongoing feature use and preferred guidance are unassessed.

## Product findings

### PROD-01 — Resume and shelves use inconsistent progress meanings

- **Owner/domain:** Product Strategist with Data and Product UI. **Severity:** Medium. **Confidence:** High in source behavior, no measured frequency. **Classification:** Confirmed code-level defect. **Independent validation:** Assigned by the lead where applicable.
- **Evidence and trigger:** `lessonProgress` returns manual completion as 100, timed lesson marks as a percentage, or rounded playhead percentage for an uncaptioned audio lesson. Home Resume labels every branch `% practised`. A cursor moved to 30 seconds of a 60-second uncaptioned lesson can produce “50% practised” with no practice. For timed Continuous, recorded listening and a stored position with no Practised marks still produce `0`, putting a partially heard lesson in **Not started**. A sufficiently short amount of real progress can also round to `0` or `100` for filter purposes. Unit shelf fixtures cover marked phrases and destination matching, but do not prove these semantics. [Progress calculation and filters](../../../../src/courses.mjs#L6-L14) · [Shelves](../../../../src/home.mjs#L14-L17) · [Resume label](../../../../src/app.mjs#L75-L80) · [Playback coverage](../../../../src/player.mjs#L233-L237) · [Current shelf tests](../../../../tests/home.test.mjs#L14-L22)
- **Root cause and impact:** One display statistic combines status, playhead and practice. It doubles as a categorisation rule through which same-looking lessons can mean different things. Default Continuous can create an honest record of listening that the browse view makes hard to find. Treat this separately from PB-02 (per-session calculations), with one shared set of definitions.
- **Remedy:** Establish distinct concepts from existing data: whether meaningful activity occurred (measured listening or explicit practice), a resumable position, earned practice marks and intentional completion. Let labels match the surface. Preserve view simplicity, current data and explicit completion preferences. Use actual activity and exact completion rules for filters; reserve rounding for display. Verify existing saved category rule behavior and migration semantics before adjusting stored filters. **Effort:** 1–2 engineer-days after agreement on definitions. **Risk:** Moderate, since course Continue and saved progress filters share the current helper. **Dependencies:** PB-02 progress definitions, Data/UX agreement, and ARC-01 relation integrity.
- **Acceptance and required checks:** With synthetic timed and untimed lessons, verify seek-only versus played coverage, marked and unmarked listening, deliberate completion, close/reload and older snapshots. Resume opens the saved position without autoplay. A pure seek leaves earned practice unchanged. Compare Home shelf membership with its See all destination and Course Continue targets. Verify a 199/200 marked timed lesson remains eligible for continuation after rounding for display. Explicit completion survives source replacement.

### PROD-02 — Empty session review changes the practice mode and queue

- **Owner/domain:** Product Strategist with Playback and UI. **Severity:** Low. **Confidence:** High in source path. Browser sequence unrun. **Classification:** Confirmed code-level defect. **Independent validation:** Recommend checking with PB-01 because both concern filter ownership.
- **Evidence and trigger:** The summary always offers **Review difficult**. It closes the summary and sets Difficult selection before testing whether any phrases are eligible. In Continuous, `setSelection` changes the mode to Manual for any non-all selection. A learner completing a session with no Difficult marks receives the empty review message after mode and queue have changed. The normal Practice Options path validates a nonempty selection before applying it. [Summary and handlers](../../../../src/app.mjs#L163-L166) · [Options validation](../../../../src/app.mjs#L371) · [Review action](../../../../src/app.mjs#L384) · [Engine selection](../../../../src/player.mjs#L166-L171)
- **Root cause and impact:** The completion shortcut has a separate mutation path with preconditions checked too late. Recovery requires navigating to Options to clear the empty selection or restore the intended mode. The shortcut should explain when there are no marks to revisit.
- **Remedy:** Reuse one validated selection transition. When no marks are eligible, suppress or neutralise the action without changing mode, queue or position. Preserve the valid direct route and ordinary choice of speaking mode. **Effort:** 0.5 engineer-day with PB-01. **Risk:** Low if a valid review still selects the first Difficult cue in source order. **Dependency:** Playback's eligibility/mode contract.
- **Acceptance and required checks:** From a Continuous completion with no Difficult marks, an unavailable review action leaves mode, selection and playhead intact and communicates the reason. With two marks, Review difficult starts the intended review at the first mark, retains both flags and leaves the other cues unmarked. Verify equivalent zero-result guard in Practice Options and correct behavior after switching back to Continuous.

4. **Test focused usability improvements before expanding scope.** Where a learner struggles to find one of the existing modes, test a short explanation in current Options or the first practice entry point. Ask a returning learner to choose a previously heard lesson, replay one phrase, mark it Difficult and resume a later session. Count actions, blocked steps and unexpected state changes in disposable test runs. Start with currently supported features, no telemetry collection and no inferred speaking proficiency. Consider broader IPA coverage only if specific repeated lookup misses affect practice and a suitably licensed source is available. Keep host dependencies minimal.
