## E2-F1 · Use a selected phrase in a fresh message

From **Practice options**, activate **Try a new conversation** for a selected phrase. **Take a phrase into a new conversation** gives a new situation. A teammate expects tomorrow’s meeting to be online. It will be in the studio instead. Ask them to bring the draft. Give one update in your own words. ## E4-F1 and E4-F2 · Try a comfortable opening off-app

Print or copy this card into an offline note.

> **Before a call**
>
> What is the one thing you want someone to take away?
>
> **Plan silently.** Pick the important detail and where a pause might help.
>
> **Say it once if comfortable.** Use your ordinary speaking voice. For example, “The draft is due on Friday. We make the decision on Monday.” Put the card down and give the next message in your own words.
>
> **Skip or rest.** If your voice feels tired or hoarse, choose silence or rest. Leave at any point. Stop speaking if it hurts, feels strained or takes increasing effort.

Compare usual preparation, silent planning and one comfortable spoken rehearsal across similar low-stakes openings. Vary the order and use a different brief message each time. Silence, rest or a skipped occasion are always available. Keep perceived ease separate from whether a willing listener can recover the intended detail and next action. Leave listener understanding unknown in a solo trial. Prefer ordinary preparation or silent planning if it serves as well. Consider an interface only if the owner finds the card repeatedly useful and identifies an access problem the paper or offline note cannot solve.
