Recommend The Listening Room

Try the same jobs

Start each prototype at its opening screen. A fresh tab gives you a new session.

  1. Speak now. Give a colleague the new draft date and ask for a reply.
  2. Continue a reference. Open Move a deadline, deliberately play the saved phrase, then replay or advance.
  3. Find and keep something. Search for deadline, add it to favourites, then find your collection.
  4. Compare a take. Listen to the reference, make an example take and switch between the two. Finish with a clear next action.
  5. Return. Find the session in the activity record and return to its reference or start another speaking turn.
