## What the previous exercise achieved

The audit set out to make the current product reliable. It traced playback faults, owner identity, mutation paths, snapshot compatibility, focus and browser release coverage. Its individual lane reports and independent review connect failures to shared contracts. The remediation subsequently fixed real risks, including private cache isolation, account sync, exact cues, import and recovery, capture lifecycle and queue/render cost. The previous final stage had 257 passing Node checks. The speaking-prompt increment reached 262 and verified two small practice experiments technically. Hosted and physical evidence was explicitly outstanding. The work was ambitious within reliability and narrow in its strategic remit. Architecture and product reports did consider reuse, simplification and consolidation. Their comparisons primarily concerned the implementation already on screen. The audit could establish whether playback, progress and recovery did what they claimed. It did not compare end-to-end practice models on the same job, explain how the product should help a speaker formulate fresh language, or test different content and navigation assumptions. It offered no participant evidence that the product changed speech. Reporting centred on findings and risk reduction, so the user-facing gains and the remaining strategic decisions were harder to see. These are gaps in scope, method and communication. The verified repairs are constraints and reusable assets for the next decision. ## The product to aim for

Echo is a private communication practice studio for a person who wants to sound clear, flexible and expressive in everyday English. The current owner values chosen British voices, short opportunities between work tasks, control of the phrase and awareness of how a message lands. A new user can speak one original fictional update immediately. A returning user can continue a personally meaningful reference in the same place. A learner who wants to explore a sound or delivery can hear an accurately bounded model, choose one focus and use it in another message. Listening, repetition, optional recording and unaided speaking have distinct purposes.

The product makes a useful start cheap, lets someone stop after one turn, and makes another visit easy without obligation. A later unfamiliar cue before replay can test whether an expression or a contrast can be retrieved. The app can display where someone left off and what they deliberately did. Speech improvement requires evidence from a new task. Design for quiet use, privately owned media, retained queue position, plain language, varied accessibility needs, and failure paths that preserve the speaker's next useful action. ## Current and future gaps

| Dimension | Current baseline | Aspirational experience and gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose and audience | The library and phrase player are the dominant objects. A separate Home cue supports original speech. | Entry points describe what a person wants to do now. Chosen references remain an equal direct start. |
| Learning method | Listen, repeat, mark, compare a recording, or open a separate transfer cue. Saved marks and time mainly describe activity. | A voluntary bridge links hearing to an unfamiliar message. Delayed retrieval and listener understanding are tested separately. |
| Information architecture | Home, library, search, collections, player, settings and sheets divide discovery, administration and practice. | A primary Practice destination, a predictable Library for discovery/management and a small History surface give each job one starting place. Test whether global search and Collections belong inside Library before moving them. |
| Mobile concentration | A persistent player, queue, swipes, exact cues and IPA are powerful. Mode names, option sheets and another interruption can hide the purpose of a step. | One salient action in short practice. The detailed player remains reachable with its state intact. Touch, keyboard, screen reader, enlargement and Back work through full journeys. |
| Content | User supplies links, captions, ZIP imports, optional aligned audio and corrections. A missing or imprecise embed complicates preparation. | Original text and a small, explicitly cleared native sample path offer quick practice. Preserve personal media and label what each source can deliver. Compare preparation cost before investing in automation. |
| Architecture and data | Existing browser modules, one Worker, owner-scoped snapshots and private audio support reliable personal use. ## Pattern research and applicability

The following product-maintained sources and standards were read on 25 November. They support design patterns and technology contracts. The team made no comparative usability or outcome claims from them.

| Source | Transferable decision |
| --- | --- |
| AntennaPod's official Android materials and screenshots distinguish library management from playback tools and show navigation between subscriptions and listening. | A curated Library can hold source management and search while a persistent playback surface serves the listening job. Keep the actual Echo transition and queue behavior under test. [AntennaPod](https://github.com/AntennaPod/AntennaPod/tree/develop) |
| Anki's author describes active recall and spaced review. | Try one separately authored message later, before exposing the model. The app can omit scheduling logic unless voluntary reuse and transfer warrant it. Flashcard recall evidence does not quantify spoken transfer. [Anki manual source](https://github.com/ankitects/anki-manual/blob/main/src/background.md) |
| wger documents editable routines and detailed tracking. | A short voluntary practice sequence can save planning decisions. Echo needs a much lighter result and can forgo prescribed weights, targets or a polished scoring dashboard. [wger](https://github.com/wger-project/wger) |
| Loop documents offline access, optional reminders, flexible frequency and export. | Allow return without a daily obligation. Plain recovery and user-owned history are more defensible than a streak, reminder service or opaque rating. [Loop](https://github.com/iSoron/uhabits/tree/dev) |
| Obsidian describes Sync as a distinct capability alongside locally held work. | Distinguish unlock, local working state and explicit sync. Echo's shared-device ownership constraint makes a cold offline library unlock a separate product and privacy decision. [Obsidian Sync documentation source](https://github.com/obsidianmd/obsidian-help/blob/master/en/Obsidian%20Sync/Introduction%20to%20Obsidian%20Sync.md) |
| W3C's minimum target-size guidance covers 24 CSS pixels and spacing exceptions. HTMLMediaElement exposes a media clock but asynchronous seeking and readiness. | Keep Echo's more comfortable 44px target for primary mobile actions and validate zoom. Native media reports the clock and actual seek outcome. It does not provide a perfect authored phrase boundary. [WCAG 2.5.8](https://github.com/w3c/wcag/blob/main/understanding/22/target-size-minimum.html) · [MDN](https://github.com/mdn/content/blob/main/files/en-us/web/api/htmlmediaelement/index.md) |

Direct commercial help centres and research domains returned proxy errors where attempted, including Apple, Spotify and Headspace. Public upstream sources were available on GitHub. No account-based competitor walkthroughs, comparative participant studies, or brand reproductions were undertaken. Learning literature and provider policy have separate provenance and limits in the relevant specialist reports.
