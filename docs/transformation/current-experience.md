# Echo Studio current experience

UX and mobile inspection of `work` at `2b9b8fe` on 25 November 2026. A person can open a short, original speaking cue with one action, or resume a prepared lesson and play with two. The primary empty Home action leads toward supplying material. The harder part of the experience is knowing what to practise, preparing a reference, using it in a new message, and knowing what to try next. [Empty Home](evidence/home-empty-320-dark.png) · [First speaking cue](evidence/speaking-cue-320-dark.png)

## Evidence and counting method

I used the rendered app and its local Worker with three disposable Chromium 156.0.8078.79 Playwright runs for first use, returning practice and media, plus a separate narrow-screen and tablet check. I started at 320×568 in dark mode and 390×844 in light mode, then checked native audio at 844×390 in landscape, 820×1180 in tablet portrait and 1440×900 on desktop in dark mode. The fixtures used an empty account and four fictional lessons, a course and a collection. The timed SRT, WAV and cover were generated for these checks. Microphone results came from a simulated browser device. The returning Home was opened in a fresh context with saved fixture data. [Browser fixture](../../tests/browser_fixture.py)

An action means one meaningful activation or field entry, including selecting a local file when applicable. Counts start from the screen named in each row. They exclude waiting, typing character by character, reading, optional setup and permission recovery. Decision counts estimate choices about route, material or setup. They exclude predetermined controls, entry of a supplied input and the work of forming a spoken response. I observed navigation, control positions, text, focus and native playback. I did not measure speaking onset, learning outcomes, thumb comfort, next-day engagement, network speed, screen-reader speech or physical-device behavior. The fixture blocked providers and service workers. Earlier handoff evidence covers cold-owner and history behavior for the two prompt dialogs separately. [Prompt verification](../product/speaking-prompt-handoff.md#local-verification-on-25-november-2026)

## Mobile and visual observations

- At both 320×568 and 390×844, the native transcript-led player and its transport fit the viewport without document scrolling. The Back and options controls were 44×44 CSS px in both sizes. The native transcript viewport increased from 288×356 to 358×604. In the 844×390 sample the smaller 528×192 transcript left room for the transport. These are layout measurements in Chromium. [Player at 390px](evidence/player-390-light.png)
- At 320×568, the Practice sheet’s **Try a new conversation** control occupied y496–543, **Finish session** y543–590 and **Record yourself** y691–735 at the starting scroll position. The last control is below the fold and the finish control crosses it. Echo Mirror’s multiple playback rows preserve their information by scrolling vertically at that width. Placement across opposite corners and the bottom makes the controls easy to distinguish on screen and gives a concrete reach hypothesis for a handheld test. [Practice options at 390px](evidence/practice-options-390-light.png) · [Mirror at 320px](evidence/mirror-320-dark.png)
- At 820×1180 with the complementary queue open, hit testing confirmed the queue intercepts the centres of Next, the Queue toggle and Compare. Play remained reachable. The same panel at 1440×900 sat alongside the player. A middle-width layout needs either reserved space for the panel or modal behavior with clear exits and no hidden actionable controls. [Tablet queue](evidence/tablet-queue-820-dark.png) · [Desktop queue](evidence/desktop-queue-1440-dark.png)
- The measured screens retain a recognizable identity through the type hierarchy, rounded interaction surfaces, and lesson-specific player tints across dark and light appearances. The player has a strong focal point. Home’s search, grouping and import architecture still surface content operations prominently, and long sheets combine goals, preferences and maintenance. The walkthrough did not quantify contrast across arbitrary cover art or motion comfort. [Empty Home in dark](evidence/home-empty-320-dark.png) · [Player in light](evidence/player-390-light.png)

## Design inputs for the next decision

1. Compare starts with the same tasks before moving navigation. A first-time speaker should be able to reach a usable cue in one deliberate action. Someone with a source should be able to resume the same paused cue and Play in two. Test which presentation makes both intents easy to find on a narrow phone. [Design alternatives](design-concepts.md)
2. Connect one selected phrase to one optional new speaking task. The alternative needs to preserve the quiet reading route, private media and scope of playback controls. Observe whether people use their own words without another decision about capture or setup. Test an unfamiliar later message separately from the in-session repetition. [Learning model](product-learning.md)
3. Explore treating preparation as a Library job before committing to the information architecture. Validate direct-file demand, the effort to supply timed captions and where someone expects to find a queue or saved set. Compare whether an alternative removes a preparation decision alongside its click count. [Add choices](evidence/add-material-320-dark.png) · [Architecture and data](architecture-data.md)
4. Keep the two verified component issues visible in evaluation. Assert that the timed-text field has its accessible name and that interactive queue/transport controls remain reachable at intermediate widths. Test real-device input, enlargement, focus after Back and failure recovery on the full proposed journey. [Quality and trust](quality-trust.md)

The reliability audit established the guardrails for owner boundaries, resume, media, focus, and import. This walkthrough provides a baseline for comparing different approaches to practice. Adoption, comprehension in natural use and any improvement in speech remain questions for participant evaluation. [Verified remediation](../audits/2026-11-25-923d203/roadmap.md)
