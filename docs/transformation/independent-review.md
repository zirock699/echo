## What the earlier work established

The prior audit and remediation improved reliability within the existing reference-led product. The strongest results are owner isolation, compatible and recoverable snapshots, explicit transport and cue behavior, a bounded queue and a complete offline shell. Architecture and product reviewers already identified useful simplifications and an explicit preserve list. Those results should constrain and enable the next design. The audit’s scope and evidence took the reference-led journey as the starting point. Its finding-by-finding reports made user outcomes and open product decisions harder to see. Comparisons of distinct practice journeys and evidence of improvement in unfamiliar speech remained open. The E1 and E2 prompts provide usable comparison routes today. [Prior preservation decisions](../audits/2026-11-25-923d203/product-disposition.md) · [Verified remediation](../audits/2026-11-25-923d203/roadmap.md) · [Speaking prompt evidence](../product/speaking-prompt-handoff.md)

The new proposal is materially broader. It treats a communicative intention as a unit of practice alongside a timed phrase, considers different places to enter and resume, separates practice boundaries from the playback clock, and distinguishes activity from transfer. It also considers preserving the present player, keeping an exercise on paper, using a prepared favourite and changing storage only where its guarantees fail. Each of those simpler routes can win the relevant comparison. [Vision and gaps](strategy.md) · [Learning alternatives](product-learning.md) · [Architecture alternatives](architecture-data.md)

## Independent corroboration of T4

I ran the provided disposable Chromium fixture with a connected Pause action and added an actual native Resume check in the fresh tab. The supplied run first timed out while Playwright waited for a replaced transport node. Using the current connected button invoked the same Pause route. Only state PUTs were interrupted. Tab B changed only the theme in its older copy. The owner’s shared record became light theme and **0 seconds**. After both tabs closed, a newly verified tab recovered that copy. Its decoder resumed paused within 0.03 seconds of zero. The disposable server remained at revision 1 and position zero. The Data Engineer observed the same sequence with a longer checkpoint. ## Design and governance

The concepts make distinct choices about control. The reference-led model gives a prepared owner direct authority over playback. The guided model could lower planning cost on an unprepared visit and introduce new decisions about the offered activity. The communication-studio model makes intention explicit and can add a decision before speaking. Combining the first two is credible as a discovery candidate. The Design Critic and I agree that the first comparison can use the current Home, with the bridge and navigation changes judged separately. Keep the goal vocabulary only where it aids a concrete turn. Judge against the simplest current path with equivalent tasks and an equally clear exit. [Concept comparison](design-concepts.md) · [Independent design critique](design-critique.md)

I inspected rendered exhibits for two direct starts on mobile and the existing 820 px queue over player controls. In the current standalone prototype at 390 × 844 in dark mode, an empty start opened a cue in one activation and a prepared reference reached simulated Play in two. Browser Back from a visible transfer kept the reference paused and restored the invoking link from both Practice and Library. I also opened the sample History cue independently. The broader current-app walkthrough gives observed actions and estimated choices. The proposed measures should distinguish time to a usable screen, time to an independently formulated utterance, success on a later message and voluntary use. Trials using people’s speech, devices and private content have separate evidence and authority requirements. [Current experience](current-experience.md) · [Standalone prototype](practice-prototype.html)

The six operating modes now specify purpose, writing authority, evidence and decision gates. UX research and mobile assessment share the existing UX role. Principal design coordinates through the lead, with a separate critic and independent strategic reviewer. The reviewed `.codex` configuration and fourteen role definitions parsed with unique names, descriptions and instructions. I observed the assigned native child task IDs. The assignment ledger identifies generic role execution in this host. Standalone named-role loading remains unverified.
