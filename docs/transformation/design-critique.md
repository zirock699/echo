## Three viable experiences

| Direction | Strength and failure mode | When to prefer it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A · Reference-led** | The clearest visual hierarchy for a prepared learner. The resume item shows the lesson and phrase, and the player groups Replay, Play and Next near the phrase metadata. The figure’s smallest actions are the queue and new-message entry. Treat their hit areas and names as design work. On the pictured Listen screen, **Speaking prompt** competes with recently used material. An empty state needs an equally legible start. Replay supports precision and imitation. Without an optional new message, this journey gives little evidence about using an expression independently. The current player makes this the cheapest model to sustain. | Prefer A if ordinary visits mostly start with a known source, a prominent prompt only distracts, and prepared listening or imitation is itself the valued outcome. Keep an original prompt within reach and preserve a text entry when media cannot play. |
| **B · One small practice** | The clearest immediate purpose for someone with no material. **Say what changed** and an unmistakable end work well together visually. **Today** can suggest a recurring obligation, and the alternate **Resume project update** has a quiet position. Existing Home already reaches a speaking cue in one action. The hypothesis is that more owners will find and take it. Keep the model optional and hidden for an unfamiliar attempt. A single bundled original cue is cheap. A rotating supply of believable situations, optional models and daily-state rules is recurring work. | Prefer B if arriving unprepared is a repeated barrier and people voluntarily take more useful speaking turns while prepared users still resume just as directly. Compare one fixed cue and a desk card before funding a sequence, reminders or any dated assignment. |
| **C · Communication studio** | **Make a change clear** gives a concrete reason to practise an emphasis. It can help an owner choose a suitable method. In the wireframe the three goal rows compete with each other and Resume is absent, although the concept specifies it. **Find an expression** and **Start comfortably** leave more to interpret, and the second screen asks for another choice. Long or enlarged labels need stacked, accessible targets. Goal names, links to references, method variants and later review each add maintenance. | Prefer C only if people repeatedly know their communicative intention and a small, understood method list reduces real selection effort or helps achieve it. First try one method within an existing task. Keep comfort an owner preference and leave diagnosis, proficiency ratings and automatic skill history out of the comparison. |
| **Hybrid · Practice plus precise reference** | The two-row Practice surface keeps both starts legible. The model-free entry takes one action and the prepared reference takes two through simulated Play. The optional bridge is visible on the reference and hides the model during the new message. Equal-looking rows could still weaken a prepared owner’s visual cue or leave a first-time speaker unsure what **original update** means. The mock Library and History are too small to test where Search, Add, Collections or marked phrases belong. One visible handoff reuses current prompt work. Simultaneously changing navigation, player layout and session storage would multiply dependencies and obscure the comparison. | Prefer the smallest hybrid if each entry is independently found and used, the optional handoff helps a real practice goal, and regular playback and material finding remain as direct. Retain present Home or switch to the simpler pure model when the trial favors it. |

The mock’s cream and charcoal surfaces, large Georgia text and restrained gold controls give each cue a focal point. The three wireframes compare hierarchy with a shared visual treatment. Preserve that control in the study. Use Echo’s existing functional language and player hierarchy for the first comparison, then test the editorial cue typography on its own if it adds value. The dark reference render’s two left-aligned 46 CSS px actions are distinct. Reaching Echo’s actual Replay, Queue and Play controls among artwork and other metadata needs its own check. ## Revised prototype and open interaction decisions

- **Context and focus.** The initial Library exit lost its origin, and a generic Finish offered two competing destinations. The revised specimen returns to Library, sends a completed Library transfer to its same sample reference with Library still selected, and gives the History exercise a single History exit. Both explicit return and browser Back from the transfer show simulated paused Phrase 4 and focus **Try a new conversation**. Browser Back after an explicit Library reference return reaches Library. The empty fixture opens its cue directly. A keyboard skip link precedes the section links, and new activity headings take focus. [Prototype](practice-prototype.html)
- **Short viewport.** Both starting choices fit at 320 × 568. In the revised light rendering, the first cue’s Finish spans y514–560 and its centre is reachable. The transfer Finish starts at y576 and needs scrolling. At 844 × 390 the transfer Finish spans y358–404 and its centre is reachable. At 640 × 360 both reference actions are within y279–325, and the transfer Finish needs scrolling to reach its centre. The phone footer follows content and leaves actions unobstructed. The enlarged 320 px transfer remained within the viewport width and Finish was reachable after substantially more vertical scrolling. Put the main action and exit within comfortable reach, then verify with browser zoom, both hands on real phones, landscape, safe-area insets and assistive technology. [Prototype](practice-prototype.html) · [Current mobile observations](current-experience.md#mobile-and-visual-observations)
- **Completion.** The mock uses **Finish** and a second contextual exit. For a one-turn, unrecorded exercise, compare a single **Done for now** return with keeping the reflection on its own screen. Keep a first-screen exit available for abandonment. The return to empty Practice lands on the heading. Require Back, Escape where the surface is modal, and visible exits to agree on the invoking context and focus. [Prototype](practice-prototype.html) · [Existing prompt handoff](../product/speaking-prompt-handoff.md)
- **Real reference handoff.** A stable sample phrase and a paused label are interaction cues. The shipped contract also needs the same source, selected phrase, cursor, queue, playback settings, unsaved Practice selections, collection origin, and ordinary checkpoint behaviour. Cancel pending starts and require explicit Play. Keep the original cue available in the public shell and allow transfer from an already authorised reference when its media is unavailable. Enforce owner checks before using private library state online or offline. Verify the current transcript label and tablet queue findings in the relevant slice. Reuse the established progress, capture and transport boundaries. ## Evidence that changes the decision

1. **Start and resume.** Compare current Home, the two-start treatment and an equivalent desk card with comparable original cues and rotated order. Start with both empty and populated libraries. Record unprompted route discovery, purposeful interactions, abandoned starts and time to the first independent turn. Keep the current one-action cue and two-action prepared Play as floors. Separately observe finding familiar material and a marked phrase. A promotion of History or combination of Search and Collections needs its own evidence of faster or clearer access. [Current walkthrough](current-experience.md#walkthroughs) · [Design comparison](design-concepts.md#proposed-direction-for-approval)
2. **Use beyond repetition.** Compare equal-time replay alone with replay plus one changed message. The sample Thursday line and a move from online to the studio demonstrate a change in situation. To test a method, choose one transferable feature beforehand, such as making the corrected detail prominent. Rotate unfamiliar prompts of similar difficulty. On a later day before replay, ask for another short update and, when available, ask an unaware willing listener to name the changed fact and next action. Keep a useful original turn, ease, voluntarily coming back and an intelligible unfamiliar message as distinct observations. Choose the simpler route if the result ties or the desk card suffices. New native materials need a specific missing example and the separate rights and preparation decision. [Product and learning evidence](product-learning.md#discovery-sequence-and-decisions) · [Roadmap](roadmap.md#t3--make-references-easier-to-prepare-and-trust)
3. **Scope the first delivery.** T1 can specify the contrast using the current cues. Open T2 only for the smallest verified focus or state-handling gap in the selected journey. A copy or hierarchy change can use the existing flow. Keep concurrent-write recovery in T4 independent, and retain the option to leave delayed retrieval off-app after T5.
